# Channel logo sources

The PNG channel logos in `italy/` come from the
[`tv-logo/tv-logos`](https://github.com/tv-logo/tv-logos) project and are kept
locally so catalog artwork does not break when a remote image host changes.
The upstream project describes the images as high-resolution logos for dark
backgrounds and requests attribution on redistribution.

The SVG files for Sky Cinema Stories, Sky Sport Basket, DMAX, Food Network,
HGTV, Discovery, Comedy Central and DAZN are sourced from Wikimedia Commons;
their file pages identify the respective broadcasters or the listed Commons
authors. Channel names and marks remain the property of their respective
owners.
