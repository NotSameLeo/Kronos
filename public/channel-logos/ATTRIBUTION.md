# Channel logo sources

The PNG channel logos in `italy/` come from the
[`tv-logo/tv-logos`](https://github.com/tv-logo/tv-logos) project and are kept
locally so catalog artwork does not break when a remote image host changes.
The upstream project describes the images as high-resolution logos for dark
backgrounds and requests attribution on redistribution.

The SVG files for Sky Cinema Stories, Sky Sport Basket, DMAX, Food Network,
HGTV, Discovery, Comedy Central and DAZN are sourced from Wikimedia Commons;
their file pages identify the respective broadcasters or the listed Commons
authors. Channel names and marks remain the property of their respective
owners.

The refreshed PNG files for the Sky Cinema and Sky Sport families, Comedy Central, Euronews,
Mediaset Infinity, Mezzo, MotoGP, Prime Video, 20th Century Studios and
American Dad are raster exports of the corresponding authentic marks hosted
by Wikimedia Commons. The Telelombardia, Smart Vision TV, Alma TV, Èlive
Brescia, ACI Sport TV and The Pet Collective files come from the broadcasters'
official websites. The Euro TV artwork comes from the broadcaster's official
App Store listing. All images preserve their original aspect ratio; Kronos
does not redraw or stretch these marks.

The channel artwork for Adriano Celentano, Alberto Sordi and Alessandro Siani
uses unaltered photographs hosted by Wikimedia Commons. The Aldo, Giovanni e
Giacomo artwork uses a photograph published on the trio's official website.
These replace the former generated 24/7 emblems; the catalog crops neither the
source photographs nor the authentic channel marks.

FailArmy and People Are Awesome use current artwork published on their official
websites. Canale 7 uses the broadcaster's official SVG rasterized without
altering its proportions. Mediaset Italia uses the high-resolution authentic
mark catalogued on Wikimedia Commons and cross-checked against the official
broadcaster website.

Formula 1 event and onboard slots use the current Formula One mark published
by Formula One Management and catalogued on Wikimedia Commons. The same real
mark is deliberately shared by the language, data and driver-feed variants.

RSI LA 1, RSI LA 2, TV2000, Rakuten TV, NASA TV, FIFA+, FashionTV and QVC use
their authentic vector marks rasterized from Wikimedia Commons without
altering proportions. Sportitalia, Explorer HD Channel, RTL 102.5, Videolina
and San Marino RTV use the high-resolution `tv-logo/tv-logos` source artwork.
The exact-name entries for Peppa Pig, 51 Radio TV, Reteveneta and TeleMia use
the broadcaster artwork indexed by the public `iptv-org` channel database;
no fuzzy name matching is used.
